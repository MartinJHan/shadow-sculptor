Shadow Sculptor

A lamp shines on your sculpture from where you are standing. Turn the sculpture until its shadow fills the dark shape on the wall.

  • Gold: your shadow covers the target.
  • Red: your shadow spills outside it.
  • Dark: target not yet covered.

You have 90 seconds. Each lock-in scores up to 1000 points for accuracy (under 80% match scores nothing), plus up to 50% bonus for speed. Sculptures get more complex as you go. One try per sculpture.
